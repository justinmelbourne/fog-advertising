SF Fog RFC Brand Canon • Rev 4 Rulings

Before & After Visual Comparison

Following rendered side-by-sides on phone and desktop (Justin, 2026-09-28), these rulings govern all geometry, hover behavior, focus indicators, and surface tokens.

Ruling 01

Geometry • Square Corners Everywhere & No Circles

border-radius: 0 is the single law. Avatars, cards, buttons, badges, and markers are square. Circles are completely retired.

Before (Rev 3 / Mixed)
Pill Tag (Radius 20px)
50% Cir
Circle timeline pin / dot
Legacy Defects:
  • Mixed 2px on buttons, 4px on cards, 20px pills on badges.
  • Circular 50% avatars and bullet dots contradicted the sharp editorial brand.
After (Rev 4 Canon)
Square Tag (Radius 0)
0 Square
Crest star marker (or square)
Rev 4 Standard:
  • border-radius: 0 on every component. Verified by speccheck.
  • Avatars/photos are square. Markers use the filled crest star glyph (icon-star).
Ruling 02

Surface Matrix • Hover Comes from the Surface

No synthetic hover tints (all --fog-*-hover retired). On light surfaces, primary Fog Blue hovers to Pitch Navy (#00243C).

Before (Synthetic Tints) Hover over buttons to test
Legacy Defects:
  • Hover states were invented tints (e.g. Light Blue on white fails contrast at 2.85:1).
  • Pale background washes created inconsistent component-scoped colors.
After (Surface Matrix) Hover over buttons to test
Rev 4 Standard:
  • Primary button fills with Pitch Navy (#00243C) on hover/press.
  • Secondary button has navy outline, filling with solid navy and white text on hover.
  • Contrast ratio is a massive 15.9:1 (white on Fog Dark Blue).
Ruling 03

Surfaces • One Permitted Tint (--fog-gray-50: #F1F2F3)

No second grayscale. Panels and card grounds use --fog-gray-50, where Fog Blue passes contrast at 4.80:1.

Before (Ad-hoc Grays)

Ad-hoc Ground (#F8F9FA)

Legacy card with 4px radius and unmanaged grey.

Legacy Defects:
  • Stylesheets grew fragmented off-whites (#F8F9FA, #F0F4F8, #E6EAEB).
  • Grey text used arbitrary #666 or #555 instead of token copy color.
After (Canon: --fog-gray-50)
Card Ground (--fog-gray-50)

Clean Editorial Surface

Strict 0 radius, 1px hairline border, Fog Blue left indicator.

Rev 4 Standard:
  • --fog-gray-50 (#F1F2F3) is the sole permitted panel/card tint.
  • Fog Blue on --fog-gray-50 passes contrast at 4.80:1.
Ruling 04

Accessibility • The Double Focus Ring

Replaces the 40% alpha ring (which composited to 1.84:1, failing WCAG) with a high-contrast 2px white / 4px Fog Blue double ring.

Before (Single Alpha Ring)
Legacy Defects:
  • 40% alpha Fog Blue composited to #99C5E2 on white.
  • Contrast ratio was 1.84:1, severely failing WCAG 3:1 focus-appearance rule.
After (Double Ring)
Rev 4 Standard:
  • 0 0 0 2px var(--fog-white), 0 0 0 4px var(--fog-blue)
  • Inner white gap isolates the control, outer Fog Blue boundary provides crisp 5.38:1 contrast.
Ruling 05

Typography • Weight by Role (Futura PT)

Display 700 (hero) • H1 800 (no hero) • H2 700 • H3 600 (sentence case) • Buttons 700 • Body 400 at 16px.

Before (Ad-hoc Weights & Negative Tracking)
ALL HEADINGS HEAVY 800
H3 ALSO SHOUTING ALL CAPS

Body text was 17px at weight 500 with muddy #555 coloring.

Legacy Defects:
  • Negative tracking compressed Futura's natural geometry.
  • H3-H6 were uppercase, causing visual shouting in editorial sections.
After (Rev 4 Canon Hierarchy)
EYEBROW 600 • 0.18EM TRACKING
DISPLAY 700 / H2 700 BOLD
H3 in clean sentence case at Demi 600

Body copy is Book 400 at 16px (1rem), no tracking, line-height 1.6, in near-black (#141718).

Rev 4 Standard:
  • Display 700 (hero) / H2 700 with +0.05em tracking and line-height 1.05.
  • H3 in sentence case at Demi 600. Body at 16px (phones never zoom).
  • strong is Bold 700 for a word or two, never a full sentence.
